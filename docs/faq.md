# FAQ

## Pressing C does nothing

The keybind only works while a calculator is in your main hand, your off hand, or an accessory slot. It does not look in the rest of your inventory. Also check **Controls** for a key conflict with another mod.

## Why does 2 + 3 × 4 give 20?

The calculator works from left to right, like a basic pocket calculator. See [How it calculates](./guide/calculator#how-it-calculates).

## Can other players hear my calculator?

No. The tones only play for you.

## Does opening the calculator pause the game?

No, not even in singleplayer. Find a safe spot before doing long sums.

## Can I turn the sounds off?

Yes. Press the `♪` key under the keypad. It stays muted until you quit the game. See [Muting](./guide/music#muting).

## Does it work on a server?

Yes. Install the mod on the server and on every client.
