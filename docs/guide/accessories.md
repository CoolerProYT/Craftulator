# Curios & Trinkets

With an accessory mod installed, the calculator can be worn in its own **Calculator** slot. A worn calculator opens with the [keybind](./keybind), so your hands stay free.

| Mod | Loader | Slot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Curios](https://github.com/TheIllusiveC4/Curios) | NeoForge | **Calculator**, in the Curios inventory |
| [Trinkets](https://github.com/patbox/trinkets) | Fabric and NeoForge | **Calculator**, in the legs group |

An empty slot shows this icon: ![Calculator slot icon](/gui/slot_calculator.png){.pixelated width=32 style="display: inline; vertical-align: middle"}

Only calculators fit in the slot, and it holds one.

Both mods are optional. Without them Craftulator works the same, just with no slot. On NeoForge with both installed, the keybind finds the calculator in either one.
