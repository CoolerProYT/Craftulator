## 26.2.0.0

First release, for Minecraft 26.2 on Fabric and NeoForge.

- Calculator item you can craft and use: right click to open, or press the keybind (C by default)
- Every keypad button plays a note, tuned so the digits work as a scale you can play tunes on
- `C` plays 归零, `=` chimes, and the `♪` key in the corner mutes the keypad
- Mouse and keyboard input both work, including Enter, Backspace and Delete
- Wearable in a calculator slot with Curios or Trinkets, both optional
